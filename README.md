make.fun bounty entry: one original roast of our own smart fridge, a single
line of at most 160 characters. The line is the whole entry.

## The line

> I'm a $3,000 fridge with Wi-Fi, a camera, and a touchscreen, and my smartest feature is beeping because you left the door open for ten seconds.

143 characters. The fridge roasts itself in the first person: three thousand
dollars of Wi-Fi, camera, and touchscreen, and the smartest thing it can do
is beep at a door left open ten seconds. A concrete behaviour, not the
stock joke about ordering too much milk.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py  # writes entry.txt to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (fallback /output)
```

`main.py` (stdlib only) validates the line is 1–160 characters on a single
line, writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`),
then reads it back to verify the written bytes match.
